=== gemini-3.5-flash | How is syphilis transmitted? | 8.7s
Syphilis is transmitted through sexual contact with infectious sores, called chancres. This can happen through direct contact with these sores on the genitals, anus, rectum, lips, or mouth, or through broken skin. Mothers can also pass syphilis to their unborn baby during pregnancy, and it can be spread through blood transfusions. 

Syphilis is not spread by touching everyday surfaces like doorknobs or tables. Using condoms correctly helps reduce the risk of transmission.

Please seek professional care at a health facility for personal medical decisions and STI testing.

=== gemini-3.5-flash | What is HPV? | 9.3s
Human papillomavirus (HPV) is a common sexually transmitted infection. It refers to a group of 200 known viruses that can affect the skin, genital area, and throat. 

Most sexually active people will be infected at some point, usually without symptoms. In 90 percent of people, the body controls the infection, and it goes away on its own without treatment. However, infection with high-risk HPV types can cause genital warts or cancers, including cervical, anal, penile, vulvar, vaginal, and mouth/throat cancers. Prophylactic vaccination can help prevent these cancers.

Please visit a health facility to consult a healthcare professional for personal medical decisions.
